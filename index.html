I really enjoy linguistics, worldbuilding, and computer science. Initially, I liked Minecraft because of its sandbox nature. This kindled a like for creative things like LEGO, drawing, and so on, but Minecraft stayed the consistent thing I liked since it was easy to pick up and leave it from time to time. Eventually, I wanted to make my own creative video games, and so I found Scratch. Scratch gave me a whole world of knowledge related to computer science, and jump forward to today and still love it.
